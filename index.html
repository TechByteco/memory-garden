"Why do students forget 70% of what they study within 7 days?"

🧬 Biology: LTP & Synaptic Consolidation

Study creates temporary hippocampal traces. Without reactivation, proteins degrade and microglia prune connections. Revising at the 40–60% inflection point triggers Long-Term Potentiation (LTP), transferring knowledge to the neocortex permanently.

📐 Maths: Differential Equations

Retention satisfies dR/dt = −R/S, solved by R(t) = e−t/S. Each revision multiplies S by k > 1, flattening the derivative. The result: exponentially longer intervals between required revisions.
