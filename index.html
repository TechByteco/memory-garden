What is Memory Garden and who built it?

Memory Garden is an interactive educational web app designed and developed by Lujain. It merges cognitive neuroscience (Long-Term Potentiation) with exponential decay calculus to make student forgetting visible in the form of wilting 3D plants.

How does it help Tamil Nadu State Board (Samacheer Kalvi), CBSE, NEET & JEE aspirants?

Instead of re-reading textbooks randomly, Memory Garden monitors each chapter's memory strength (\(S\)). When retention falls into the 40%–60% inflection window, the plant asks for water. Revising at this exact instant triggers maximum dendritic spine consolidation, cutting study time by up to 50% while boosting exam scores.

How does the 3D Water Bucket feature work?

When you finish an active recall session (flashcard or Feynman prompt), you receive a 3D water bucket. Drag or move it near your plant; the bucket tilts, pours a sparkling water stream with realistic sound effects, and your plant visibly straightens, grows fresh leaves, blossoms, and returns to 100% health.

Is Memory Garden free for students in Tamil Nadu?

Yes, Memory Garden is 100% free and open-access. It runs directly in Google Chrome without account registration or app downloads, saving all progress securely in your local browser storage.

"Why do students forget 70% of what they study within 7 days?"

The brain evolved to prune unrepeated stimuli. Memory Garden fuses neuroscience with exponential calculus to make forgetting visible — and fixable. Designed & built by Lujain.

🧬 Biology: LTP & Synaptic Consolidation

Study creates temporary hippocampal traces. Without reactivation, proteins degrade and microglia prune connections. Revising at the 40–60% inflection point triggers Long-Term Potentiation (LTP), transferring knowledge to the neocortex permanently.

📐 Maths: Differential Equations

Retention satisfies dR/dt = −R/S, solved by R(t) = e−t/S. Each revision multiplies S by k > 1, flattening the derivative. The result: exponentially longer intervals between required revisions.
